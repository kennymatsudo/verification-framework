# Kiosk requirements

Agreed with the cafe owner. Prices are in cents; the screen shows them as dollars.

- KR-1 The kiosk page shows every menu item with its name and price: Espresso $3.00, Latte $4.50, Croissant $3.75.
- KR-2 Tapping "Add" on an item puts one of it in the cart; tapping it again raises the quantity by one.
- KR-3 The total shown on the page equals the sum of each cart line's price times its quantity.
- KR-4 Placing the order stores it with the same lines and total the customer saw, and the page shows the stored order's number.
- KR-5 Placing an order with an empty cart is refused: the page shows "Add something first" and nothing is stored.
- KR-6 Sales tax will be shown as a separate line. The rate depends on the location and is not decided yet; until then the total is the pre-tax total.
